What This Module Teaches

System Design interviews are collaborative reasoning exercises. There is rarely one correct architecture. Your job is to clarify the important requirements, choose a simple starting design, identify where it breaks, and explain the tradeoffs behind each change.

A strong design is a chain of justified decisions, not a pile of fashionable technologies.
